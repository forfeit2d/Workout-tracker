Boerum Hill Beginner Split (4-Day)

Your 4-Day Beginner Program

Designed specifically for machine usage at PureGym Boerum Hill. Alternate between full-body strength building and cardiovascular conditioning.

Day 1 Strength

Full-Body Workout A

Focus on major machine pushes, pulls, and leg power.

  • Leg Press Machine
  • Seated Chest Press
  • Lat Pulldown Machine
  • Seated Shoulder Press
  • Abdominal Crunch Machine
Day 2 Cardio

Run Session 1: Walk/Run Intervals

High-efficiency treadmill intervals to build aerobic stamina.

⏱️ Structure: 5m Warmup Walk → 10x (1m Run / 1m Walk) → 5m Cooldown Walk (30 mins total).

Day 3 Recovery

Rest / Active Recovery

Light walking around Brooklyn, foam rolling, or stretching.

Day 4 Strength

Full-Body Workout B

Slight variation focusing on row movements and core stability.

  • Leg Press Machine
  • Seated Cable Row
  • Seated Chest Press
  • Seated Shoulder Press
  • Abdominal Crunch Machine
Day 5 Cardio

Run Session 2: Steady Endurance

Continuous treadmill effort to strengthen cardiac output.

⏱️ Structure: 20–25 minutes at a comfortable conversational jog speed.

Days 6 & 7 Recovery

Weekend Rest Days

Muscle tissue rebuilds while resting. Prioritize hydration and 7–8 hours of sleep.
